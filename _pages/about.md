I'm a Senior ML & Systems Engineer at Klover.ai, where I build multi-agent LLM systems and AI-driven workflows.

My path into AI runs through systems. Over about 15 years I've worked across telecom, power and performance engineering at Intel, and applied AI/ML. At Intel I built AI inference and telemetry pipelines, which shaped how I think about models today: not just what they can do, but how they run, what they cost, and how they behave in production.

These days I'm most interested in:

- **Multi-agent architectures** — how LLM agents coordinate, hand off work, and stay reliable at scale
- **Efficient inference** — scheduling and energy-aware execution for agent pipelines
- **Full-stack AI applications** — taking an idea from model to something people actually use

This blog is where I write about what I'm building and learning, including side projects like **TradeDeck**, an AI-powered stock trading dashboard, and **Ctrl-Alt-Feel**, a multi-agent emotion engine for executive coaches.

I hold an MS in Computer Science from San Jose State University and a Professional Certificate in Machine Learning and AI from UC Berkeley.
